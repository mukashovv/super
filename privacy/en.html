Privacy Policy

Version of 23 November 2026 · Effective upon publication

SuperTable helps you manage your personal finances.

Data and purposes

We process your email, account details, accepted document version and subscription status to provide sign-in, account recovery and access. We use the income, expenses, debts, payments, goals and notes you enter to save your ledger and calculate totals. Infrastructure providers may process connection and error data for delivery, security and diagnostics.

Storage and providers

The local trial ledger stays in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the site, jsDelivr delivers a software library and Supabase handles authentication and cloud records. Your browser stores your language and session. Signing out does not delete cloud records. WhatsApp receives messages you choose to send. Renewal messages may include your account email. Providers may process data outside your country.

Protection and your rights

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, but cannot guarantee absolute security. You can request access, corrections, a copy or deletion as described under Manage and Delete Data. We do not use your records to provide individual investment advice.
