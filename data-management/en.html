Manage and Delete Data

Version of 23 November 2026 · Effective upon publication

Edit or delete an entry

Open its month, select the entry and edit or delete it. Review the new monthly total.

Clear a local ledger

Keep a copy of anything you need. In your browser’s site data settings, remove the data for supertable.mukashov.com. This clears the local ledger and settings in that browser. Cloud records remain available.

Request a copy or delete your cloud account

Email the owner from your registered address. Use “SuperTable data copy” or “Delete my SuperTable account” as the subject. Say if you want a copy before deletion. Do not send passwords, login codes or transaction details.

We will verify account ownership. After confirmation we will remove the account and linked records from the active database and email you the result. Access will end. We will explain if any information must be retained by law.
